## Research

**Reinforcement learning for trauma ICU care (Bachelor's thesis, ongoing)**
Advisor: Prof. Vibhor Pandhare, IIT Bombay.
I built a simulator on MIMIC-IV trauma ICU data and I'm training RL policies that jointly minimise mortality and resource cost. The question I care about is how to allocate scarce ICU resources sensibly when every decision has a clinical cost.

**Generative design research at Adobe (summer 2026)**
Diffusion-based generative design with multi-modal conditioning, an agentic LLM pipeline, and custom evaluation metrics. A paper from the team is in preparation.

## Course projects

**Retinal vessel segmentation (CS 736, Medical Image Computing)**
Compared classical graph-based methods (minimum spanning tree, Dijkstra) against deep learning models (U-Net, Attention U-Net, ResNet34 U-Net) on the DRIVE dataset, with Focal + Dice loss and k-fold cross-validation. Written up as a report and defended in a viva.

**Billing system integrated with a hospital information system (Clinical Data Management)**
Group project. I worked on system design and architecture: the database schema, system modules and ER diagram, covering ER modelling, transaction integrity, and open-source HIS platforms such as Bahmni and OpenEMR.

**Neighbour Nudge (IE 619, Combinatorial Game Theory)**
A game I designed myself, plus an All-Small version of it, analysed with the tools from the course.

**Service roads in Powai (DE 341, urban design)**
Group study of the Suvarna Temple Service Road, extending the hardware / software / practice place model to a "thing" most people only walk past.

**Frontier AI governance (AI policy course, ongoing)**
Literature review on regulating frontier AI, with a focus on containment failures and loss of control.
