## Thirteen years, and counting

I've been learning **Bharatnatyam for 13 years**. It has become one of the main ways I understand myself: the discipline of the adavus, the storytelling in abhinaya, the way a single line of a song can be danced a dozen different ways.

## Dance and a day job (that was also an IIT)

Nobody tells you how to schedule a rehearsal around a problem set deadline, so I invented a system: practice in small, protected blocks rather than waiting for free time (there is no free time at IIT), and treat dance as the thing that makes the rest of the week work, not as the thing that competes with it. Through school and through college, it never disappeared from my timetable.

## The break

Right now I'm on a pause. My health asked for a time-out, and for the first time in over a decade, dance isn't a part of my week. It's strange, and some days it's hard.

But a pause isn't an ending. I'm already planning the return: slowly, kindly, and with a lot more respect for my body. When I'm back, this is where I'll say so.
