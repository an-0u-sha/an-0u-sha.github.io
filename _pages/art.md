## The short version

I draw. I doodle on everything: margins, napkins, the back of problem sets. At some point the doodling got serious, and I started learning art properly at **In the Name of Meera**, where I worked my way from pencil to paint and eventually to full canvases.

## Where art meets the rest of me

I think the doodling is the same impulse as the research: noticing how things are structured, and wanting to make the invisible visible. It's also why I like medical imaging and generative models so much. Both are, in a way, ways of drawing.
