I notice cities with my senses first. The energy of pedestrians at a crossing, a lane under monsoon flooding, the contrast between a street vendor's stall and the glass tower behind it.

That curiosity is why I ended up studying a service road in Powai for an urban design course: the Suvarna Temple Service Road, a place most people only use to get somewhere else. I'm interested in what a road like that is made of (its hardware, its software, and the daily practices that hold it together).
